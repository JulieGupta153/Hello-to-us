Description

The story begins with Farhan Qureshi and Raju Rastogi, two struggling engineers, learning from their rival Chatur that they have a clue to the whereabouts of their long-lost friend, the eccentric genius Rancho. The film then unfolds in a series of flashbacks to their college days at the Imperial College of Engineering (ICE) where they met Rancho, a free-spirited student who prioritized understanding concepts over rote memorization.
